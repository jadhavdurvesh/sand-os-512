# 14 — Originality and naming policy

Thryvane must define its own register/group semantics, instruction encoding, arithmetic, control flow, memory translation, privilege transitions, event model, boot contract, executable format, and ABI.

Existing architectures may be studied and cited. Materially derived ideas should be acknowledged and evaluated; proprietary code, text, diagrams, or specifications must not be copied.

Thryvane, Syr, Syrrack, Vael, Vaelchain, Duskloom, Threadglass, and Rhuneforge are provisional project terms, not claims of trademark uniqueness.

Originality is demonstrated by the specification, emulator, conformance tests, assembler, and ABI—not by names alone.
